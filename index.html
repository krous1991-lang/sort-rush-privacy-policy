Data handled by Google AdMob is subject to Google's policies and retention practices. We do not control the retention of information collected by third-party advertising services.

Your choices

You can manage advertising and privacy choices through your Android device settings and the privacy controls made available by Google, where available. You can also review Google's privacy resources linked above.
